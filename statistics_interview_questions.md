# Conceptual Statistics Interview Questions

High-priority questions aligned with common StrataScratch and FAANG-style interviews.

## Central Limit Theorem

1. State the Central Limit Theorem. What does it say about the sampling distribution of the mean?
2. Why is the CLT useful even when the underlying data is not normal?
3. Roughly how large does n need to be for the CLT approximation to be reasonable for a skewed distribution?

## p-value & Hypothesis Testing

4. What is a p-value? (State Farm / Amazon style)
5. Would your interpretation of a p-value change if the dataset were much larger (e.g., 3 million rows)? Why or why not?
6. Explain Type I and Type II errors. What is power?
7. Walk through the steps of a hypothesis test.
8. When would you use a one-tailed versus a two-tailed test?

## Confidence Intervals & Sample Size

9. How does the margin of error change with sample size?
10. How many more samples do you need to reduce the margin of error from 3% to 0.3%? (Google style)
11. Write the formula for a large-sample confidence interval for a proportion.

## Multicollinearity

12. What is multicollinearity and why is it a problem in linear regression?
13. How would you detect it? (Correlation matrix vs VIF)
14. What VIF values typically indicate a problem?

## Descriptive Statistics & Outliers

15. When would you prefer the median over the mean? (Facebook age example)
16. How do you detect whether a new observation is an outlier? (Microsoft style)
17. Compare Z-score, IQR, and modified z-score (MAD) methods.

## A/B Testing

18. What must be true of an A/B test design for the results to be trustworthy?
19. How do network effects complicate A/B tests on social platforms? What designs address them?
20. Explain Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) and how you would check for it.
21. What is CUPED and why is it useful?

## Probability

22. Difference between independent and dependent events. Give examples.
23. Combinations versus permutations.
24. State Bayes’ theorem. Contrast Bayesian and frequentist approaches.
