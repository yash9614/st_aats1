# Statistics Interview Questions

Organized from Codebasics math & stats materials. Use alongside the concept notes.

## Beginner

1. Imagine you're conducting a survey about a new product but only collect responses from people in a shopping mall. What type of bias might this introduce and why?
2. What does the Law of Large Numbers state, and why is it important?
3. What is the difference between a parameter and a statistic?
4. Difference between a normal distribution and a standard normal distribution? Why convert data to standard normal?
5. What is the Standard Normal Distribution, and how is data distributed within ±1, ±2, and ±3 standard deviations?
6. What is a hypothesis? Explain null vs alternative with a real-life example.
7. One-tailed vs two-tailed test? Give examples.
8. What is Alpha (α) in hypothesis testing?
9. When is the median more suitable than the mean?
10. Characteristics of a bell-curve distribution?
11. What are Degrees of Freedom?

## Intermediate

1. Survey of 20 people gives average screen time 5.4 h; survey of 2,000 gives 5.0 h. Which concept explains why the larger survey is closer to the true average?
2. What is the Central Limit Theorem? Why is it important?
3. What happens to the mean, variance, and standard deviation when moving from a population distribution to the sampling distribution of the sample mean?
4. Testing a new blood pressure medication: describe Type I and Type II errors, trade-offs of α, and how changing α affects β in a high-stakes setting.
5. Customer churn dataset is highly imbalanced (8% churn). What statistical challenges arise and how would you address them (evaluation, error types, adjustments)?
6. E-commerce wants to compare two checkout designs for purchase completion rate. How would you design the experiment and what factors matter in analysis?
7. Three promotional email campaigns; record purchase (Yes/No). How to test if campaign type impacts purchase behavior? (test, assumptions, multiple comparisons, unbalanced groups)
8. Why is the t-test not used for a two-sample test of proportions?
9. Different types of t-tests and when to use each?
10. How to determine if two samples have equal variance?
11. How would you estimate average daily screen time of teenagers across the country?
12. Two datasets have the same mean and standard deviation. What additional descriptive measures would you use and why?
13. How does sample size affect the reliability of standard deviation and variance?
