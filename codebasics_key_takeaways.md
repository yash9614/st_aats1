# Codebasics Math & Stats — Key Takeaways

Distilled from official Codebasics takeaway PDFs. These points reinforce the practical notes and interview questions.

## Descriptive vs Inferential Statistics

- **Descriptive statistics** summarize and describe data, providing an overview of its main characteristics.
- **Inferential statistics** involve making predictions or drawing conclusions about a population based on a sample.

## Measures of Central Tendency

| Measure | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| Mean | Synonym for average |
| Median | Middle value of an ordered dataset; average of the two middle values if the count is even |
| Mode | Most frequently occurring value |

## Percentiles & Quartiles

- A **percentile** ranks a value within a dataset, indicating what percentage of the data falls below or equal to that value.
- 25th percentile = lower quartile (Q1)
- 50th percentile = median (Q2)
- 75th percentile = upper quartile (Q3)

## Measures of Dispersion

| Measure | Definition | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Range | Maximum − Minimum | Reflects total spread; sensitive to outliers |
| IQR | Q3 − Q1 | Less influenced by outliers; robust measure of spread |

Quartiles Q1, Q2, and Q3 correspond to the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles.

## Distributions

- **Distribution** refers to the arrangement or spread of different values.
- **Normal distribution** features most values clustered in the middle, forming a bell-shaped curve.
- **Probability distribution** estimates the likelihood of various outcomes based on chance.
- **Discrete distribution**: values occur in specific steps or groups (e.g., counts).
- **Continuous distribution**: values can occur anywhere within a range (e.g., height, weight).

## Sampling & Bias

- **Sample bias** occurs when a sample is not representative of the population.
- **Participation bias** occurs when individuals who choose to participate differ significantly from those who do not.
- **Survivorship bias** happens when we only look at success stories and ignore failures.
- **Language bias** is the influence of language on perception and decision-making.

Trial participants and samples should be representative of the target population.

## Hypothesis Testing Foundations

- **Hypothesis testing** is a statistical technique for decision-making or inferring population characteristics.
- **Null Hypothesis (H₀)**: Represents a statement of no effect or no difference; serves as the benchmark.
- **Alternative Hypothesis (Hₐ / H₁)**: Proposes a new effect or difference, challenging the null hypothesis.
